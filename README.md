# TMSK | README

### **Overview**
**Takam Mising Sí:sang Kébang (TMSK)** is a digital platform for a youth-led collective dedicated to the Mising community. It integrates traditional governance values with modern advocacy, focusing on cultural preservation, political rights, and academic documentation.

---

### **Core Structure**
The site is organized into three distinct **Wings**:

* **Political Wing:** Focuses on sovereignty, rights, and socialist values.
* **Cultural Wing:** Focuses on the legacy of Abotani and digitizing Mising folktales.
* **Research Wing:** Focuses on building a permanent knowledge base of Mising history and geography.

---

### **Technical Features**
* **Modern UI:** Responsive design with a custom "Ticker" for community slogans.
* **Theme Engine:** JavaScript-powered dark/light mode toggle with local storage persistence.
* **Navigation:** Smooth-scroll internal linking and external redirects to specialized wing pages.
* **Community Integration:** Direct CTAs for Discord and Instagram-based membership applications.

---

### **Project Info**
* **Established:** May 9, 2026.
* **Stack:** HTML5, CSS3 (Variables/Grid/Flexbox), Vanilla JS.
