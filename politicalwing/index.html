★ Our Goals

What We Fight For

The Political Wing of TMSK is driven by five core commitments — to every Mising person, in every corner of Northeast India. Identity. Equality. Justice. Self-Determination.

01
To Unite

To unite all Mising people for a stronger collective identity and a shared future — one voice, one people, one struggle.

02
To Empower

To empower our people socially, economically, culturally, and politically at every level of society.

03
To Fight

To fight against injustice, exploitation, discrimination, and the ongoing displacement of the Mising people from their ancestral lands.

04
To Build

To build a society based on equality, solidarity, and socialist values — where no Mising person is left behind or left voiceless.

05
To Defend

To defend our land, language, culture, and rights — protecting the future generations of the Mising nation from erasure.

★ Our Symbols

The Imagery We Carry

Every symbol we carry is a declaration. The imagery we use tells the story of our resistance, our hopes, and our unbreakable spirit.

✊
Raised Fist
Resistance & Unity

The raised fist is our most powerful symbol — the collective strength of a people who refuse to be silenced. It represents solidarity across every Mising community.

★
Red Star
Socialist & Revolutionary Ideals

The red star stands for our commitment to socialist values — equality, collective welfare, and the rights of workers and peasants. A guiding light for our movement.

⚔
Crossed Swords
Defense of Identity & Struggle

The crossed swords represent the Mising people's determination to defend their identity, land, and culture against all forms of exploitation and erasure.

☀
Rising Sun
Awakening, Hope & Rebirth

The rising sun over the Mising homeland symbolises the awakening of our people — a new dawn of political consciousness, cultural rebirth, and collective hope for the future.

★ Key Demand

Sixth Schedule

The Mising community needs the Sixth Schedule — not merely as a political demand, but as a matter of survival for our identity, land, language, culture, and future.

Protection of Land & RightsAutonomous Councils under the Sixth Schedule would give our communities the power to protect our land from encroachment and our rivers from exploitation.
Preservation of Language & CultureSpecial provisions for Mising language instruction, script preservation, and cultural institutions — ensuring our heritage survives for future generations.
Better Representation & DevelopmentGenuine self-governance through tribal councils — so that Mising people decide their own development priorities, not outsiders.
Safeguarding Identity for Future GenerationsA community without its language and culture slowly loses its very existence. The Sixth Schedule is a shield against erasure.

We are not asking for separation — we are asking for protection, respect, and recognition of our indigenous roots. A community without its culture and language slowly loses its existence.

— Socialist Mising Nationalism
