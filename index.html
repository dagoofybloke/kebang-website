Who We Are

The Kébang for a New Generation

A youth-led collective rooted in the traditions of the Mising people, building forward.

The Takam Mising Sí:sang Kébang (TMSK) is a youth organisation dedicated to preserving and advancing the culture, rights, and knowledge of the Mising people of Assam and Arunachal Pradesh, Northeast India.

We take our name from the Kébang — the traditional democratic council of the Mising people — a model of collective decision-making and community governance that has guided our ancestors for generations. We bring that spirit into the digital age.

TMSK operates through three active wings: a Political Wing that advocates for Mising rights, a Cultural & Content Creation Wing that preserves Abotani's heritage, empowers a dedicated group of content creators actively promoting Mising culture, and engages Mising youth — and a Research & Documentation Wing that builds and preserves the Mising knowledge base for future generations.

What is a Kébang?

The Kébang is the traditional village council of the Mising people — a democratic institution where elders and community members gather to resolve disputes, make collective decisions, and uphold community values. TMSK revives this spirit for a new generation.

Join the Kébang

TMSK is open to Mising youth who share our commitment to culture, rights, and knowledge. Applications are handled directly via Instagram.

We welcome dedicated individuals who want to contribute to the preservation and advancement of the Mising people's culture, political rights, and documented knowledge.

Applications are reviewed by the founding team. To apply, simply send a direct message to one of our contacts on Instagram — introduce yourself, state which wing you're interested in, and briefly explain why you'd like to join.
