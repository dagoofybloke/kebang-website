Who We Are

The Kébang for a New Generation

A youth-led collective rooted in the traditions of the Mising people, building forward.

The Takam Mising Sí:sang Kébang (TMSK) is a youth organisation dedicated to preserving and advancing the culture, rights, and knowledge of the Mising people.

We take our name from the Kébang — the traditional democratic council of the Mising people — a model of collective decision-making and community governance that has guided our ancestors for generations. We bring that spirit into the digital age.

TMSK operates through three active wings: a Political Wing that advocates for Mising rights and socialist values, a Cultural Wing that preserves Abotani's heritage and engages Mising youth, and a Wikipedia Wing that builds the Mising knowledge base on the world's largest open encyclopedia.

What is a Kébang?

The Kébang is the traditional village council of the Mising people — a democratic institution where elders and community members gather to resolve disputes, make collective decisions, and uphold the values of the community. TMSK revives this spirit for a new generation.

The political voice of TMSK — advocating for the rights, identity, and socialist values of the Mising people of Northeast India.

Wing II
Cultural Wing

Abotani's Legacy

Preserving and celebrating the culture of the Mising people — from the folktales of our ancestors to the screens of our youth.

@apongrizz
Preservation of Abotani's Culture

Documenting, preserving and celebrating the rich cultural heritage rooted in the mythology and traditions of Abotani — the ancestral figure of the Tani peoples.

Digitalisation of Folktales

Recording and digitising the oral folktales, myths, and stories of the Mising tribe so they are preserved and accessible for generations to come.

Promoting Traditional Heritage

Bringing Mising traditions — from the Ege textile to the Dobur Uie festival, from Apong to the Yoksa Gai motif — to wider audiences through digital platforms.

Engaging Mising Youth

Connecting with the young Mising generation — on Instagram, Discord, and beyond — to ensure our culture lives not just in memory, but in active practice.

Wing III
Wikipedia Wing

Mising Knowledge on the Open Web

Expanding what the world knows about the Mising people — one Wikipedia article at a time. Our goal is to build a comprehensive, accurate record of Mising culture, history, and people on English Wikipedia.
