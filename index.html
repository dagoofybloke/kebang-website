Preamble · Our Purpose

Why TMSK Exists

We are Mising youth who refuse to watch our language, culture, and identity quietly disappear. Here is what we stand for.

01
Unity

One Home for All Mising Youth

Mising youth are scattered across Assam, Arunachal, and beyond — with no single platform to call our own. TMSK is that platform. One place to connect, organise, and act together.

02
Cultural Revival

Revive What We Are Losing

Our language is fading. Our traditions are disappearing. Many Mising youth today know more about Assamese culture than their own. This has to change.

Being proud of your tribe doesn't make you backwards — it makes you powerful. The Bodo people organised, asserted their identity, and won the 6th Schedule and a place in the 8th Schedule. We can do the same.

Being Mising is not a disadvantage. It is our strength.
03
Structure

Three Wings, One Spirit

TMSK runs through three autonomous wings — Political, Cultural & Content Creation, and Research & Documentation. Each wing operates independently; its members belong to their wing, not to TMSK directly.

What ties us together is not hierarchy — it is a shared love for the Mising people.

Who We Are

The Kébang for a New Generation

A youth-led collective rooted in the traditions of the Mising people, building forward.

The Takam Mising Sí:sang Kébang (TMSK) is a youth organisation dedicated to preserving and advancing the culture, rights, and knowledge of the Mising people of Northeast India.

We take our name from the Kébang — the traditional democratic council of the Mising people, a model of collective decision-making and community governance that has guided our ancestors for generations. We bring that spirit into the digital age.

What is a Kébang?

The Kébang is the traditional village council of the Mising people — a democratic institution where elders and community members gather to resolve disputes, make collective decisions, and uphold community values. TMSK revives this spirit for a new generation.
