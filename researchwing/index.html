Our Work

What This Wing Does

The Research & Documentation Wing is the knowledge arm of TMSK — dedicated to ensuring Mising culture, history, and identity are researched, preserved, and accessible for generations to come.

Cultural Research

Investigating and documenting Mising traditions, oral histories, rituals, language, and customs — preserving the depth of Abotani's legacy in written and digital form.

Digital Archiving

Digitising and archiving Mising knowledge — folktales, historical records, biographical accounts, and cultural practices — so they survive the passage of time on the open internet.

Wikipedia Editing

Creating and improving articles about the Mising people on English Wikipedia — ensuring the world's largest encyclopedia accurately represents our culture, geography, and history.

Wikipedia Editors

Our active Wikipedia contributors — building and expanding the Mising presence on English Wikipedia.
