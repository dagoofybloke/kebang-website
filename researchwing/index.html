Our Work

What This Wing Does

The Research & Documentation Wing is the knowledge arm of TMSK — dedicated to ensuring Mising culture, history, and identity are researched, preserved, and accessible for generations to come.

Cultural Research

Investigating and documenting Mising traditions, oral histories, rituals, language, and customs — preserving the depth of Abotani's legacy in written and digital form.

Digital Archiving

Digitising and archiving Mising knowledge — folktales, historical records, biographical accounts, and cultural practices — including books currently locked away in university libraries, made free for all.

Wikipedia Editing

Creating and improving articles about the Mising people on English Wikipedia — ensuring the world's largest encyclopedia accurately represents our culture, geography, and history.

I
Profile
Researchers & Academics

Researchers — especially those whose work focuses on Mising culture, language, history, or related fields. University fellows, academics, or graduate students who can bring rigour and depth to our documentation efforts are particularly valuable to this wing.

II
Profile
Deep Internet Researchers

People who are willing and able to conduct sustained, focused research on specific topics about Mising culture using online sources — tracing historical references, cross-checking facts, and compiling well-sourced summaries that can be turned into documented records or Wikipedia articles.

III
Profile
Library & Digitisation Volunteers

University fellows or individuals with access to academic or institutional libraries who can help digitise books, journals, and records about the Mising people that currently exist only in physical form. The goal is to bring this knowledge onto open digital libraries so the general public can access it freely, without barriers.

IV
Profile
Wikipedia Editors

Experienced or aspiring Wikipedia editors who can create new articles, expand existing stubs, and improve the accuracy and quality of Mising-related content on English Wikipedia — making sure the world's most widely read encyclopedia properly reflects Mising culture, people, and history.

V
Profile
Knowledge Digitisation Contributors

Anyone who can contribute to getting Mising knowledge onto the open web in any form — whether that means transcribing oral traditions, uploading out-of-print texts to Internet Archive, creating structured databases of Mising villages and geography, or any other form of digital knowledge preservation. If you care about Mising identity surviving and thriving online, this wing welcomes you.

Digital Library

Potin Kumsung

Potin Kumsung is the open digital library initiative of the Research & Documentation Wing — preserving and freely sharing books, academic material, cultural records, and rare texts related to the Mising people. More books and archival material will continue to be added over time.
