Our Work

What This Wing Does

The Research & Documentation Wing is the knowledge arm of TMSK — dedicated to ensuring Mising culture, history, and identity are researched, preserved, and freely accessible.

01 / Cultural Research
Cultural Research

Investigating and documenting Mising traditions, oral histories, rituals, language, and customs — preserving the depth of Abotani's legacy in written and digital form.

02 / Digital Archiving
Digital Archiving

Digitising and archiving Mising knowledge — folktales, historical records, biographical accounts, and cultural practices — including books locked in university libraries, made free for all.

03 / Wikipedia Editing
Wikipedia Editing

Creating and improving articles about the Mising people on English Wikipedia — ensuring the world's largest encyclopaedia accurately represents our culture, geography, and history.

Membership

Who We Need

This wing is built on scholarship, rigour, and a shared commitment to preserving Mising knowledge. We are looking for people who bring one or more of the following to the table.

I
Profile
Researchers & Academics

University fellows, academics, or graduate students whose work focuses on Mising culture, language, or history. People who can bring rigour and depth to our documentation efforts.

II
Profile
Deep Internet Researchers

People able to conduct sustained, focused online research on specific Mising topics — tracing historical references, cross-checking facts, and compiling well-sourced summaries for documentation or Wikipedia.

III
Profile
Library & Digitisation Volunteers

Individuals with access to academic or institutional libraries who can help digitise books, journals, and records about the Mising people — bringing this knowledge onto open digital libraries, freely available to all.

IV
Profile
Wikipedia Editors

Experienced or aspiring editors who can create new articles, expand existing stubs, and improve the accuracy and quality of Mising-related content on English Wikipedia.

V
Profile
Knowledge Digitisation Contributors

Anyone who can contribute to getting Mising knowledge onto the open web — transcribing oral traditions, uploading out-of-print texts to Internet Archive, building structured databases of Mising villages and geography, or any other form of digital knowledge preservation. If you care about Mising identity surviving online, this wing welcomes you.
